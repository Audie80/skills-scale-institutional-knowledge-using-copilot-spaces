# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This collection describes how OctoAcme takes customer-first work from an initial idea through delivery and continuous improvement. Use the guides below for the details at each stage of the lifecycle.

## Project management process at a glance

OctoAcme follows an iterative lifecycle. Teams begin by validating the business need, success measures, stakeholders, and available resources. Once work is approved, they plan it as prioritized, estimated increments with clear acceptance criteria, ownership, milestones, and an approach to risks and dependencies. During execution, teams track progress through regular team and stakeholder communication, project boards, quality checks, and timely blocker escalation. Releases use readiness checks, deployment verification, clear communication, and rollback planning. After delivery, retrospectives capture learnings and turn them into focused, measurable improvement actions.
